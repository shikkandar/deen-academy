# Credits

## Hadith — `assets/data/namaz-hadith.json`

Sahih al-Bukhari, complete chapters 9 "Times of the Prayers" (#522–602) and 11 "Friday Prayer" (#876–941),
Arabic text with English translation, from [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
(released into the public domain under The Unlicense; editions `ara-bukhari`, `eng-bukhari`).
Each entry links to its page on sunnah.com for verification.

## Quran — text, translation and audio (loaded at runtime)

From the [AlQuran Cloud API](https://alquran.cloud/api) (no key required; audio served from cdn.islamic.network):
Arabic text `quran-uthmani`; English translation Saheeh International (`en.sahih`); ayah-by-ayah recitations
by the reciters listed in the app; English audio by Ibrahim Walk (`en.walk`); Urdu audio by Shamshad Ali Khan (`ur.khan`).
